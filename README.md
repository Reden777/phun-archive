# PHUN Archive

An archive of the old game Phun. It was created by a Swedish university student and its former website, http://www.phunland.com, went down many years ago, albeit the 4.22 download was available at its bare link at least until ~2018 for some reason.

Eventually however, it all redirected to Algodoo's website. While Algodoo is supposedly Phun's successor and built from its code, its physics felt different to me when I tried it years back.

While http://www.phunland.com said to not mirror things, it doesn't apply anymore because the main distribution died many years ago and only Softonic/similar are left.

Win32 4.22 and 5.28 are included.
